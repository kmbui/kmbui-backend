# 🌌 Quick Tip: Theverted Vision

If you are looking at a faint star or nebula and it seems to disappear when you
look directly at it, try **Averted Vision**.

### How it works

- Look slightly to the side of the object.
- The outer edges of your retina are more sensitive to light than the center.
- This allows you to see objects up to **40 times** fainter than you can with
  direct vision!
